Laundry Day Disaster

Made just for you

The hamper blew its lid. Chase the runaway undies, and leave the stink bombs alone.

A goofy gag game. No ads, no accounts, no purchases. Nothing is saved except the settings on this device.

How to Play

Catch the runaways

  • Move the player left and right, and catch falling cartoon undies in the laundry basket.
  • On a phone, drag or slide. On a keyboard, use the left and right arrow keys or A and D.
  • Each catch builds a combo, and combos raise your score.
  • If an undie hits the floor, your combo resets.

Dodge the stink bombs

  • Stink bombs are round, green, and trailing squiggles. Do not catch them.
  • In Endless mode, three misses or stink bombs end the round.
  • Press Space or P to pause. Press Escape to leave a round.

The undies are silly cartoon characters with faces. They are not worn by anyone in the game.
